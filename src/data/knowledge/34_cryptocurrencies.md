# Cryptocurrencies: The Digital Asset Class

## What is Cryptocurrency?

Digital money that works without banks:
- Decentralized (no central authority)
- Uses cryptography (securing transactions)
- Blockchain technology (shared ledger)
- Examples: Bitcoin, Ethereum

## Bitcoin Basics

### What is Bitcoin?
Digital currency created 2009:
- Limited supply: Only 21 million coins
- Decentralized: No central bank
- Scarce like gold (hence "digital gold")
- No physical form

### How Bitcoin Works
Transactions are recorded in blockchain:
- Computers verify transactions
- Miners add transactions to blocks
- Blocks linked chronologically
- Transparent ledger everyone sees

### Bitcoin Value
Why is Bitcoin worth anything?
- Supply limited (scarcity)
- Demand from users/investors
- Network effect (more users = more value)
- Store of value narrative

## Cryptocurrency Types

### Bitcoin (The Original)
- Market cap: $2+ trillion
- Purpose: Money/store of value
- Supply: Fixed 21M coins
- Speed: Slow (10 min per transaction)
- Investment: Store of value bet

### Ethereum (The Programmable)
- Market cap: $500B+
- Purpose: Smart contracts (programs on blockchain)
- Supply: Unlimited
- Speed: Fast (seconds)
- Investment: Technology platform bet

### Stablecoins
- Pegged to dollar (USDC, USDT)
- Value stays $1
- Purpose: Trading without volatility
- Use: Move money between exchanges

### Altcoins (Everything Else)
- 10,000+ cryptocurrencies
- Most have no real value
- Speculative bets
- High risk

## Cryptocurrency Risks

### Extreme Volatility
Bitcoin 2024 price range: $40,000 - $70,000+
- 75% swing possible
- Psychological: Hard to hold
- Mathematical: $10,000 → $2,500 possible
- Cause: Speculation, few users, narrative changes

### Irreversibility
- Lose private key: Coins gone forever
- Send to wrong address: Can't recover
- Scammed: No fraud protection
- No bank to call for help

### Regulatory Risk
- Government bans possible
- Tax treatment unclear
- Rules keep changing
- Legal uncertainty

### Technical Risk
- Hacking possible (exchanges hacked before)
- Bugs in software
- Wallet vulnerability
- Self-custody is risky

### Adoption Risk
- Only 5%+ of world uses crypto
- Needs mainstream adoption to succeed
- Could stay niche forever
- Or become ubiquitous

## Cryptocurrency as Investment

### The Bull Case
- Limited supply (scarcity)
- Global adoption potential
- Digital future probable
- Early investors benefit
- From $0.01 to $70,000+ (Bitcoin)

### The Bear Case
- Extreme volatility (not investment)
- No fundamental value (why buy?)
- Tulip bulb analogy (speculative bubble)
- Transaction speed issues
- Environmental impact (mining)

### Reality
- Crypto is speculative
- Could 10x or go to $0
- No one knows
- High-risk, high-reward bet
- Possible part of future, also possible flop

## Should You Invest in Crypto?

### If You Invest
- Only gamble money you can lose
- Maximum 5-10% of portfolio (not core)
- Dollar-cost average ($100/month better than lump sum)
- Use reputable exchange (Coinbase, Kraken)
- Store safely (cold wallet better than exchange)
- Don't panic sell in downturns

### If You Don't Invest
- Not missing out on essential strategy
- Index funds ~10% long-term returns historically
- Crypto could go to zero
- Your retirement doesn't depend on crypto
- Perfectly fine to ignore

## Tax Implications

### Taxed as Property (IRS)
- Each transaction is taxable event
- Buying crypto: Not taxable
- Selling crypto: Capital gain/loss
- Trading frequently: Short-term capital gains (taxed as income ~37%)
- Trading infrequently: Long-term gains (taxed as capital gain ~20%)

**Example**:
- Buy Bitcoin: $10,000
- Sell Bitcoin: $15,000
- Gain: $5,000
- Tax (short-term): ~$1,850
- Tax (long-term): ~$1,000
- Net: $13,150

### Recordkeeping
- Exchanges report to IRS
- You need records of:
  - Cost basis (what you paid)
  - Sale price (what you sold for)
  - Date of each transaction
- Better apps: CoinTracker, TaxBit

## Cryptocurrency Scams

### Pump and Dump
- Buy unknown coin cheap
- Hype it on social media
- Price spikes
- Creators sell ("dump")
- Price crashes
- Retail investors lose

### Fake Coins
- Meme coins (created for fun)
- Celebrity coins (celebrity endorses)
- Eventually: Creators exit scam
- Investors lose 99%

### Ponzi Schemes
- FTX example: Looked legitimate
- Was actually Ponzi (new money paying old investors)
- Collapsed: $8 billion lost

**Red flags**:
- Promises of guaranteed returns
- FOMO pressure ("limited time!")
- Celebrities endorsing
- Unknown founders
- 1,000%+ gains promised

## Key Takeaway
Cryptocurrency is speculative, volatile, and risky. Bitcoin has value narrative (scarcity, digital gold), but no guaranteed value. Ethereum is programmable platform with potential. Most other coins are garbage. If you invest: 5-10% max, DCA, only risk money you can lose. If you don't invest: You're fine, not missing core strategy. Crypto could become ubiquitous or disappear. No one knows. Treat as lottery ticket, not retirement plan.
