# Tax Basics for Investors

## Tax-advantaged vs taxable accounts

- **Tax-advantaged**: 401(k)s and IRAs grow with deferred or tax-free treatment, subject to rules and limits.
- **Taxable brokerage**: You pay tax on dividends and on capital gains when you sell. No contribution limits.

## Capital gains

When you sell an investment for more than you paid, the profit is a capital gain.

- **Short-term**: Held one year or less; taxed as ordinary income.
- **Long-term**: Held more than one year; usually taxed at lower long-term capital gains rates.

Tax-loss harvesting (selling losers to offset gains) can reduce taxes but has rules and trade-offs; consider professional advice.

## Dividends

Dividends can be qualified (often taxed at lower rates) or non-qualified (taxed as ordinary income). The type depends on how long you held the stock and other factors.

## Withholding

Retirement account withdrawals are often subject to withholding. You can adjust withholding on forms like W-4 (wages) or the form provided by your plan administrator.

*This is general education only. Tax rules change; consult a tax professional for your situation.*
