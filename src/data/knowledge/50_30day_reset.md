# 30-Day Financial Reset: Your Action Plan

## Why 30 Days?

30 days = Enough time to implement basics, build momentum, see results

**Myth**: Takes years to fix finances  
**Reality**: Basics in 30 days, compounding in years

## Week 1: Assessment

### Day 1-2: Income Clarity
**Action**:
- Write down gross annual income
- Calculate monthly take-home
- List all income sources (salary, bonus, side income)
- Document: Date, amount for bonus/variable income

**Example**:
- Salary: $60,000/year gross
- Take-home: $45,000/year ($3,750/month)
- Bonus: $5,000/year (unpredictable)
- Side income: $500/month (~$6,000/year)
- **Total: $57,000/year available**

### Day 3-4: Spending Audit
**Action**:
- Review last 3 months statements (bank, credit cards)
- Categorize every transaction
- Identify top 10 spending categories
- Calculate monthly average by category

**Categories**:
- Housing (rent/mortgage)
- Utilities
- Food
- Transportation
- Insurance
- Subscriptions
- Entertainment
- Clothing
- Misc

**Example**:
- Housing: $1,500
- Food: $600
- Transportation: $400
- Insurance: $300
- Utilities: $200
- Subscriptions: $150
- Entertainment: $300
- Other: $200
- **Total: $3,650/month**

### Day 5-7: Goal Setting
**Action**:
- Write 3 goals (short, medium, long-term)
- Assign timeline to each
- Write why (motivation)
- Make goals SMART (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound)

**Examples**:
- Emergency fund: $10,000 by end of year (safety net)
- House down payment: $50,000 in 5 years (stability)
- Retirement: $500,000 by age 55 (freedom)

## Week 2: Foundation

### Day 8-9: Debt Inventory
**Action**:
- List all debts
- Interest rate on each
- Minimum payment on each
- Target payoff amount
- Total debt owed

**Example**:
- Credit card 1: $5,000 at 18%
- Credit card 2: $2,000 at 15%
- Student loan: $20,000 at 5%
- Car loan: $10,000 at 6%
- **Total debt: $37,000**

### Day 10-11: Budget Creation
**Action**:
- Calculate available income after taxes
- List expenses from day 3-4
- Identify cuts to reach 20-30% savings goal
- Create simple budget

**Simple format**:
- Income: $3,750
- Expenses: $3,000 (target)
- Savings: $750/month
- **Savings rate: 20%**

**If need cuts**:
- Subscriptions: Cut $150 (keep 1-2, drop rest)
- Dining out: Reduce $150 (meal prep)
- Entertainment: Reduce $100
- Misc: Reduce $100
- **Total cut: $500/month**

### Day 12-14: Account Setup
**Action**:
- Open high-yield savings account (4-5% interest)
- Set up automatic transfer ($750/month to savings)
- Link 401(k) if available (set contribution)
- Open Roth IRA (if eligible)

**Accounts to consider**:
- High-yield savings (Marcus, Ally, Wealthfront Cash)
- 401(k) through employer
- Roth IRA (Vanguard, Fidelity, Schwab)
- Brokerage account (Vanguard, Fidelity)

## Week 3: Debt Reduction

### Day 15-16: Credit Card Payoff
**Action**:
- Minimum monthly payment + extra
- List cards (highest interest first)
- Commit to payoff timeline
- Track progress

**Example**:
- Card 1: $5,000 at 18%
- Pay $500/month (vs $100 minimum)
- Payoff: 10 months (vs 7 years!)
- Interest saved: $3,500+

### Day 17-18: Expense Reduction
**Action**:
- Cancel unused subscriptions (call if needed)
- Renegotiate bills (insurance, phone, internet)
- Reduce discretionary spending
- Implement 30-day rule (wait before buying)

**Easy cuts**:
- Gym membership unused: Cut
- Streaming services: Keep 1, cut rest
- Coffee subscriptions: Brew at home
- Dining out: Reduce frequency
- Impulse buying: Implement 30-day pause

### Day 19-21: Debt Strategy
**Action**:
- Choose avalanche (highest interest first) or snowball (smallest balance first)
- Create payment plan
- Track progress monthly
- Celebrate small wins

**Recommendation**: Avalanche (mathematical) or hybrid (psychology boost from quick win)

## Week 4: Building Wealth

### Day 22-23: Investment Education
**Action**:
- Read articles (index funds, Roth IRA, compound interest)
- Watch 3 videos (personal finance basics)
- Understand: Why investing matters, power of compounding
- Learn: Index fund basics, low-cost vs high-fee

**Resources**:
- Bogleheads.org (free education)
- Vanguard blogs
- Khan Academy (investing basics)
- YouTube (search "investing for beginners")

### Day 24-25: Investment Setup
**Action**:
- Open accounts (if not done already)
- Choose simple index fund allocation (100% VTI for simplicity)
- Automate monthly investment
- Review allocation (goal: 70/30 stocks/bonds or 100/0 if young)

**Simple portfolio**:
- 100% VTI (total market index)
- Or: 70% VTI + 30% BND (bonds)
- Or: Use Vanguard target-date fund (auto-adjusts)

### Day 26-27: Income Optimization
**Action**:
- List ways to increase income
- Side business ideas (freelance, gig, service)
- Raise request timeline (next review)
- Part-time opportunities
- Create 30-60-90 day plan for implementation

**Ideas**:
- Freelancing (your profession online)
- Gig work (Doordash, Uber, Taskrabbit)
- Tutoring ($15-30/hour)
- Online course creation
- Selling stuff (declutter)

### Day 28-30: Documentation and Automation

**Action**:
- Create financial dashboard (spreadsheet)
- Set calendar reminders (quarterly review, annual review)
- Automate all savings/investments
- Print budget, post on fridge
- Schedule 30-day review (Day 31)

**Dashboard tracks**:
- Income: $____ (monthly)
- Expenses: $____ (monthly)
- Savings: $____ (monthly)
- Net worth: $____ (snapshot)
- Debt: $____ (declining)
- Investments: $____ (growing)

## Success Keys

✅ **Write things down** (tracking = accountability)
✅ **Automate** (removes willpower)
✅ **Celebrate small wins** (motivation)
✅ **Review regularly** (stay on track)
✅ **Be consistent** (small actions compound)
✅ **Forgive mistakes** (everyone slips)
✅ **Keep perspective** (30 days starts journey, 30 years builds wealth)
