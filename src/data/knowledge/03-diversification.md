# Diversification: Don't Put All Eggs in One Basket

## What is diversification?

Diversification means spreading your investments across many different assets, sectors, and sometimes regions. The goal is to reduce the impact of any single investment’s poor performance on your overall portfolio.

## How to diversify

- **Across asset classes**: Combine stocks, bonds, and possibly other assets (e.g., real estate funds).
- **Within stocks**: Hold many companies and industries rather than one or two stocks.
- **Geographically**: Include both U.S. and international investments to reduce country-specific risk.

## Benefits

- **Lower volatility**: When some holdings fall, others may rise or hold steady.
- **Smoother returns**: Over time, a diversified portfolio often has less dramatic swings than a single stock.
- **Risk management**: You are less exposed to one company or sector failing.

## Limits

Diversification does not eliminate risk or guarantee a profit. It can help manage risk and make it easier to stay invested during market downturns.
