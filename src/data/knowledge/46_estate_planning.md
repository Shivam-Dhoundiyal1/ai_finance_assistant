# Estate Planning: Protecting Your Legacy

## Why Estate Planning Matters

Without plan:
- Court decides (expensive, slow)
- Family fights (relationships damaged)
- Taxes maximize (less for heirs)
- Kids unprotected (if minor)
- Wishes ignored

With plan:
- Your wishes followed
- Family protected
- Taxes minimized
- Process clear
- Peace of mind

## Key Documents

### Will
**What is it**: Legal document stating who gets what

**Includes**:
- Who inherits (assets, percentages)
- Guardians for children
- Executor (person managing estate)
- Specific bequests (jewelry to daughter)

**Costs**: $500-1,500 (or DIY $150-300 online)

**Important**: Easy to update/change

### Beneficiary Designations
**What**: Direct payout to person/people

**Assets that skip probate**:
- Life insurance
- 401(k)/IRA
- Brokerage accounts (TOD transfer on death)
- Bank accounts (POD payable on death)

**Benefit**: Don't go through court, faster

**Update annually**: After marriage, divorce, births

### Power of Attorney
**What**: Document giving someone power to act for you

**Types**:
- Financial: Pay bills, manage accounts (if incapacitated)
- Medical: Make medical decisions (if incapacitated)

**Benefit**: Someone manages affairs without court

### Healthcare Proxy/Directive
**What**: Who decides medical treatment if you can't

**Includes**:
- Who decides (person's name)
- What you want (DNR or full intervention)
- Organ donation (yes/no)

**Benefit**: Your wishes followed, clear decisions

## Asset Organization

### List Assets
Create document with:
- Bank accounts (balance, location)
- Investments (brokerage, balance)
- 401(k)/IRA (custodian, balance)
- Life insurance (policy number, death benefit)
- House/property (title location)
- Car (title location)
- Valuable possessions (jewelry, art)
- Digital assets (accounts, passwords)

### Create Inventory
For each asset:
- Current value (estimate)
- Location (bank, custodian)
- Account numbers
- How titled (individual, joint, trust)
- Who should inherit

### Location Map
Tell executor/family:
- Where documents kept
- Who has keys to safe deposit
- Passwords for digital access
- List of advisors (accountant, lawyer, banker)

## Estate Taxes

### Federal Estate Tax
- 2024: Exemption $13.61M (married $27.22M)
- Only affects wealthy
- Rate: 40% above exemption
- Most people: Zero federal tax

**Example**:
- Estate: $5M
- Exemption: $13.61M
- Federal tax: $0 (under exemption)

### State Estate Tax
- Only some states have it
- Varies by state
- Affects smaller estates than federal
- California: No state tax
- Massachusetts: 3.06-16% (applies below federal threshold)

### Step-Up Basis
**Tax benefit to heirs**:
- You buy stock at $10
- You die, stock worth $50
- Heirs inherit at $50
- NO capital gains tax if sell immediately
- Saves taxes

## Minimizing Taxes

### Strategies
1. **Gifting**: Give $18,000/year per person (2024) tax-free
2. **Irrevocable trusts**: Remove assets from estate
3. **Charitable trusts**: Donate to charity, reduce estate
4. **Step-up basis**: Natural benefit (noted above)

### For Most
Estate taxes not concern (under $13.61M)
Focus: Organization + Will + Beneficiary designations

## Who Needs What

### Married, Kids, Assets Under $1M
**Minimum**:
- Will (basic, $300-500)
- Beneficiary designations
- Healthcare proxy
- Power of attorney

### Single, No Kids, Assets $100k+
**Minimum**:
- Will (basic, $300)
- Beneficiary designations
- Healthcare proxy
- Name executor (friend/family)

### Married, Kids, Assets $1M+
**Recommended**:
- Revocable living trust (more complex, $1,000-2,000)
- Will
- Beneficiary designations
- Healthcare proxy
- Power of attorney
- Possibly: Tax planning with CPA

### High Net Worth ($5M+)
**Recommended**:
- Comprehensive estate plan
- Tax attorney (complex strategies)
- Irrevocable trusts
- Charitable planning
- Multi-state considerations

## Common Mistakes

❌ **No will**: Court decides, expensive, slow  
❌ **Will only**: Probate delays (6 months - 2 years)  
❌ **Wrong beneficiaries**: Outdated designations  
❌ **Joint ownership**: Can create unintended consequences  
❌ **No healthcare directive**: Family doesn't know wishes  
❌ **No password list**: Family can't access accounts  
❌ **Assets titled wrong**: May not transfer as intended  

## Where to Start

### DIY Option (If Simple)
1. LegalZoom: Will + Trust templates ($50-300)
2. Nolo: DIY estate planning ($15-50 books)
3. State bar: Free/cheap clinic often available
4. Online services: RocketLawyer, LawDepot

**Good if**:
- Under 1M assets
- Simple family situation
- Few bequests
- No complex taxes

**Risk**: May miss issues, may be invalid in some states

### Attorney Option (If Complex)
1. Find estate planning attorney
2. Initial consultation: Often free
3. Build plan: $1,000-3,000
4. Regular updates: $200-500 each

**Good if**:
- Complex family
- Significant assets ($1M+)
- Multi-state property
- Want professional guidance
- Peace of mind important

## Digital Assets

**Often forgotten**:
- Email accounts
- Social media (Facebook, Twitter, etc)
- Online banking
- Cryptocurrency
- Cloud storage (photos, documents)
- Subscription services

**Create list**:
- Account name
- Username
- Where password kept
- Instructions (delete, memorialize, archive)
- Beneficiary (who gets/controls)

**Legally**: May need separate will for digital assets

## The Process After Death

### Immediately
- Locate will and other documents
- Executor takes control
- Notify banks, insurers (death certificate required)
- Secure property

### Within weeks
- Pay immediate bills (medical, funeral)
- Notify creditors
- Inventory assets
- Start probate (if needed)
- Tax ID for estate

### Within months
- Settle all debts
- File final tax return
- Distribute assets per will/law
- Close estate (probate closes)

### Timeline
- Simple estate: 6-12 months
- Complex estate: 1-3 years
- Varies by state laws

## Key Takeaway
Estate planning not morbid, it's loving. Protects family from court, expense, family fights. Will alone takes 6 months+ (probate). Beneficiary designations skip probate (faster). Power of attorney handles incapacity. Healthcare directive ensures wishes followed. Most need: Will, beneficiary updates, power of attorney, healthcare proxy. Costs: $500-2,000 for good plan (worth it). Updates: Every 5 years or major life event. Peace of mind: Priceless.
