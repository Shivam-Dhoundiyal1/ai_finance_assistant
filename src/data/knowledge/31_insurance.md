# Insurance: Protecting Your Wealth

## Why Insurance Matters

Insurance is risk transfer:
- You pay small amount
- Insurance company covers catastrophic loss
- Protects years of financial progress

**Example**: $1,000,000 house
- Without homeowner insurance: One fire = lose everything
- With insurance: Pay $1,200/year, fire covered
- Trade $1,200 for $1,000,000 protection

## Types of Insurance

### Health Insurance
Covers medical expenses:
- Doctor visits
- Hospital stays
- Prescriptions
- Preventive care

**Importance**: Bankruptcy risk high without coverage

### Homeowner/Renter Insurance
Covers property damage:
- Fire, theft, natural disaster
- Liability (guest injured on property)
- Mandatory if mortgaged

**Cost**: $1,000-$2,000/year
**Covers**: $300,000+ in property

### Auto Insurance
Covers car accidents:
- Liability (damage you cause)
- Collision (your car damage)
- Comprehensive (theft, weather)
- Uninsured motorist

**Mandatory**: Required by law most states

**Cost**: $1,000-$2,000/year

### Life Insurance
Covers your death:
- Replaces lost income
- Pays off debts
- Funds children's education
- Handles final expenses

**Importance**: Critical if anyone depends on you

### Disability Insurance
Covers lost income if you can't work:
- Injury or illness prevents work
- Replaces 60-70% of income
- Often through employer (free!)

**Importance**: You're more likely disabled than dead at 35

### Umbrella Insurance
Extra liability protection above other policies:
- Covers lawsuit beyond homeowner/auto limits
- $1M coverage: $150-$300/year
- For assets worth protecting

## Life Insurance Types

### Term Life (Best for Most)
Pays benefit if you die within term:

**30-year term, age 35, $1M coverage**:
- Cost: $25-50/month (very cheap!)
- Pays: $1M if you die
- Expires: At age 65 (then re-evaluate)

**Perfect for**:
- Young families with dependents
- Need big death benefit
- Budget conscious

### Whole Life (Insurance + Investment)
Pays benefit no matter when you die:

**Whole life, age 35, $1M coverage**:
- Cost: $500-1,000/month (expensive!)
- Builds cash value (investment side)
- Guaranteed payout whenever

**Only good if**:
- Want permanent coverage (beyond age 80)
- Want investment component (usually bad returns)
- Need estate planning (most don't)

**Most people**: Term is better value

### Universal Life (Variable Whole Life)
Flexible whole life:
- Adjustable premiums
- Investment component with choices
- More expensive than term

**Most people**: Still prefer term

## How Much Life Insurance?

### Simple Rule: 10x Annual Income
- Income: $100,000
- Life insurance needed: $1,000,000

### Better Rule: Calculate Needs
**Expenses you'd leave behind**:
- Mortgage payoff: $300,000
- Children's college (15 years): $200,000
- Spouse's living expenses (30 years): $1,500,000
- Final expenses: $20,000
- **Total**: $2,020,000

### Online Calculator
- LIFE Foundation: LifeHappens.org
- Free calculator
- Accounts for debts, goals, inflation

## Do You Need Insurance?

### Health Insurance
✅ **Everyone needs**:
- Mandatory (legally)
- Catastrophic cost protection
- No work-around

### Homeowner Insurance
✅ **If you own home**:
- Mandatory (lender requires)
- House is biggest asset
- Protection essential

### Auto Insurance
✅ **If you drive**:
- Mandatory (legally)
- Liability risk high
- Protection essential

### Life Insurance
✅ **If anyone depends on income**:
- Children dependent on you
- Spouse dependent on you
- Still paying mortgage
- Debts to inherit

❌ **Don't need if**:
- No dependents
- Wealthy/self-funded
- Near end of career
- Already have coverage through work

### Disability Insurance
✅ **If income is critical**:
- Young (higher disability risk)
- Support dependents
- High-income earner
- No savings for 1+ year

❌ **Don't need if**:
- Have large emergency fund (1+ year)
- Wealthy
- Near retirement
- Already covered by employer

### Umbrella Insurance
✅ **If you have assets**:
- Own home with equity
- Have investments
- Significant income
- Sued beyond car/home liability limits

❌ **Don't need if**:
- Minimal assets
- Covered by employer

## Common Insurance Mistakes

❌ **Over-insuring**: Paying too much coverage  
❌ **Under-insuring**: Not enough protection  
❌ **Not shopping**: Staying with expensive carrier  
❌ **High deductibles**: Saving premium but risking cash  
❌ **Skipping disability**: Most valuable protection young  
❌ **Buying whole life**: Expensive, unnecessary  

## Insurance Shopping Tips

### Get Quotes
- Get 3-5 quotes
- Same coverage level
- Compare prices
- Save significantly

### Bundle Policies
- Homeowner + auto: 5-15% discount
- Adding umbrella: Small additional cost
- Bundling can save hundreds

### Increase Deductibles
- Higher deductible = Lower premium
- $500 → $1,000: Save $100-200/year
- Good if you have emergency fund

### Discounts Available
- Defensive driving course: 5-10%
- Good credit: 5-25% (varies)
- New customer: 10-15%
- Bundle: 5-25%

## Evaluating Your Coverage

### Homeowner Insurance
**Got quotes?** Within $1,000-1,500?
**Deductible?** Should be $500-1,000
**Coverage?** Home replacement value (not actual cash value)
**Liability?** At least $300,000

### Auto Insurance
**Got quotes?** Within $1,000-2,000/year?
**Liability limits?** At least 100/300 ($100k/$300k)
**Collision?** If car worth $5k+, yes
**Comprehensive?** If car worth $5k+, yes
**Deductible?** $500-1,000

### Life Insurance
**Have coverage?** If dependents yes
**Amount?** 10x income minimum
**Type?** Term best for most
**Duration?** Through dependent's graduation

## Key Takeaway
Insurance protects years of financial progress. Health insurance mandatory. Homeowner/auto insurance mandatory if applicable. Life insurance essential if dependents. Disability insurance most valuable young. Term life very cheap ($25-50/month). Shop around (5-20% savings common). Bundle policies for discounts. Umbrella insurance cheap for asset protection. Don't over-insure, don't under-insure. Insurance is boring but essential risk management.
