# 401(k): Your Company Retirement Plan

## What is a 401(k)?

A 401(k) is a tax-advantaged retirement savings plan offered by employers. You contribute money from your salary, and it grows tax-free until retirement.

## Key Features

### Employee Contributions
- Deducted from your paycheck pre-tax
- 2024 limit: $23,500/year (age <50)
- 2024 limit: $31,000/year (age 50+ - catch-up)
- Reduces taxable income today

### Employer Match
**Free money!** Many employers match contributions:
- Common match: 50% of first 6% (employer adds $0.50 for every $1 you contribute up to 6% of salary)
- Example: If you earn $100,000 and contribute $6,000 (6%), employer adds $3,000
- **Always contribute enough to get full match**

### Tax Treatment

**Traditional 401(k)**:
- Contributions are pre-tax (reduce taxable income today)
- Growth is tax-deferred
- Withdrawals in retirement are taxed as income
- Pay taxes later when you're (hopefully) in lower tax bracket

**Roth 401(k)**:
- Contributions are after-tax (no tax deduction today)
- Growth is tax-free
- Withdrawals in retirement are tax-free
- Better if you expect higher tax bracket in retirement

### Investment Options
Your 401(k) offers menu of investments:
- Stock funds
- Bond funds
- Target-date funds (auto-adjust as you age)
- Money market funds

### Vesting
Your contributions are always yours. Employer match vests on schedule:
- Immediate: You own it right away
- Cliff vesting: You own 100% after X years (ex: 3 years)
- Graded vesting: You own increasing % over time

### Withdrawals

**Before age 59.5**:
- 10% penalty
- Income tax on withdrawal
- Exceptions: Disability, medical expenses, first home ($10k)

**After age 59.5**:
- No penalty
- Income tax owed
- Can withdraw as much as you want

**Required Minimum Distributions (RMD)**:
- Must start withdrawing at age 72-73
- IRS requires you withdraw minimum amount

## 401(k) Advantages

✅ **Employer match is free money**  
✅ **Tax savings**: Reduce current year taxes  
✅ **Tax-deferred growth**: Money grows without tax drag  
✅ **Payroll deduction**: Automatic (forces discipline)  
✅ **High contribution limits**: $23,500/year  
✅ **Creditor protection**: Protected in bankruptcy  

## 401(k) Disadvantages

❌ **Early withdrawal penalty and taxes**  
❌ **Limited investment choices** (vs. IRA)  
❌ **High fees possible** (some plans)  
❌ **Administrative costs**  
❌ **No flexibility**: Can't access money until 59.5 without penalty  

## 401(k) vs IRA vs Taxable Brokerage

| Feature | 401(k) | IRA | Taxable |
|---------|--------|-----|---------|
| Contribution Limit | $23,500 | $7,000 | Unlimited |
| Employer Match | Yes | No | No |
| Tax Deduction | Yes (traditional) | Yes (traditional) | No |
| Withdrawal Age | 59.5 | 59.5 | Anytime |
| Investment Options | Limited | Many | Many |
| Easiest | Yes | No | No |

## 401(k) Best Practices

1. **Contribute enough for full employer match** (minimum)
2. **Choose target-date fund** if unsure what to invest in
3. **Rebalance annually**
4. **Don't panic sell** during market downturns
5. **Review investment fees** (aim for low-cost index funds)
6. **Increase contributions** when you get raises

## Key Takeaway
A 401(k) is the easiest and most valuable retirement savings tool. Contribute enough to get full employer match, choose diversified investments, and let time work for you. Avoid early withdrawal penalties by leaving money invested until retirement.
